About Bartolome Sanggalang National High School

Committed to quality education

Barawid Street, St. John District, Guimba, Nueva Ecija, Philippines

Bartolome Sanggalang National High School (BSNHS) is a public secondary school located at Barawid Street, St. John District, Guimba, Nueva Ecija, committed to providing accessible and quality education to learners in the community. As part of the Department of Education (DepEd), the school plays a vital role in shaping responsible, skilled, and values-driven students prepared for higher education, employment, and nation-building.

BSNHS offers both Junior High School and Senior High School programs, ensuring that students receive a complete basic education aligned with national standards. The school continuously strives to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where every learner is encouraged to reach their full potential.

With dedicated teachers and staff, BSNHS focuses not only on academic excellence but also on the holistic development of its students—fostering critical thinking, creativity, discipline, and strong moral values.

Mission

BSNHS upholds the mission of the Department of Education to protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education. The school is committed to nurturing learners through effective teaching, supportive guidance, and a learner-centered environment.

Vision

BSNHS envisions producing empowered learners who are equipped with essential knowledge, skills, and values, enabling them to contribute meaningfully to their community and the nation.
